# Product Overview

## Examinr.ai Waitlist

> The official public landing page for Examinr.ai.

---

## Purpose

This repository contains the marketing website and public waitlist for Examinr.ai.

Its purpose is to communicate the vision behind the platform, introduce its value to students and educational institutions, collect early adopters, and build a community before the core product launches.

The waitlist is intentionally lightweight and should remain focused on conversion rather than functionality.

The actual Examinr.ai platform is developed separately.

---

# About Examinr.ai

Examinr.ai is a predictive AI learning platform built to help students prepare with confidence.

Rather than acting as another general-purpose AI chatbot, Examinr.ai combines predictive intelligence, educational resources, examination analysis, and institution collaboration into one intelligent study workspace.

Students don't just receive answers.

They receive direction.

---

# Repository Goals

The waitlist should accomplish five things.

1. Introduce the Examinr.ai vision.

2. Clearly explain how Examinr.ai differs from traditional AI study tools.

3. Convince students and institutions to join the waitlist.

4. Build trust and credibility before launch.

5. Prepare users for migration into the full platform.

# Product Philosophy

The waitlist is not designed to impress visitors with animations or unnecessary interactions.

Its purpose is to communicate confidence.

Every section should answer one question:

"Why should I trust Examinr.ai before it launches?"

---

# Brand Positioning

Examinr.ai is not another AI chatbot.

It is a predictive intelligence platform for education.

Where traditional AI answers questions,

Examinr.ai helps students decide what deserves their attention.

---

# Success Metrics

The waitlist is considered successful when it:

- Collects high-quality registrations.
- Clearly communicates the product vision.
- Encourages sharing.
- Builds credibility with investors.
- Creates anticipation for launch.

---

# Deployment

Production deployments are handled through Vercel.

Every change merged into the production branch should be stable and deployment-ready.

---

# Future

Once the Examinr.ai platform launches, waitlist users should migrate into the main application without creating a new identity.

The waitlist should therefore be treated as the first step of the Examinr.ai user journey rather than a temporary marketing campaign.

> **This repository is frozen in scope.**

Its responsibility is to support the waitlist experience only.

New product features should be implemented in the main Examinr.ai application repository rather than expanding the responsibilities of this project.
